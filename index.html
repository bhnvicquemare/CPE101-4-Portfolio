Course Work 2

A reverse engineering analaysis of a Bass Guitar Amplifier. Through reverse engineering, one can learn about the design and functionality of any technology or gadget they wish to analyze. This is because reverse engineering is fundamental to engineering as a whole, not just in Computer Engineering. In reverse engineering, one must consider the Engineering Design Process, which is a series of steps that engineers follow to come up with a solution to a problem. In this case, the objective is to understand how a bass guitar amplifier works, why it is needed, and how it can be improved.
